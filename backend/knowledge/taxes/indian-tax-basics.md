# Indian Income Tax Basics

## Old Tax Regime vs New Tax Regime
The Income Tax Department of India provides two distinct regimes for individual taxpayers:

### New Tax Regime (Default)
The New Tax Regime offers lower tax rates but removes most exemptions and deductions (like 80C, 80D, HRA, and LTA). 
- Income up to ₹3,00,000 is tax-free.
- A standard deduction of ₹50,000 is available for salaried employees.
- Under Section 87A, individuals with taxable income up to ₹7,00,000 pay zero tax due to the rebate.

### Old Tax Regime
The Old Tax Regime has higher tax rates but allows you to claim various exemptions and deductions to lower your taxable income.
- Income up to ₹2,50,000 is tax-free.
- Section 80C: Deductions up to ₹1,50,000 on investments like PPF, ELSS, EPF, LIC premiums, and home loan principal repayment.
- Section 80D: Deductions for medical insurance premiums up to ₹25,000 for self/family, and an additional ₹50,000 for senior citizen parents.
- HRA (House Rent Allowance) and LTA (Leave Travel Allowance) exemptions can be claimed.

## When to choose which?
If your total deductions (80C, 80D, HRA, home loan interest, etc.) exceed ₹3.75 Lakhs, the Old Regime is generally more beneficial. If you do not have significant tax-saving investments or rent receipts, the New Regime is more profitable due to its lower slab rates.
