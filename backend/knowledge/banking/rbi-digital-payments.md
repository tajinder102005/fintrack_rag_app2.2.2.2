# Digital Payments and RBI Guidelines

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) oversees and regulates the digital payment infrastructure in India, ensuring safety, security, and efficiency.

## Digital Payment Methods

### UPI (Unified Payments Interface)
A real-time payment system developed by NPCI that facilitates inter-bank peer-to-peer and person-to-merchant transactions.
- **Standard Limit:** The general transaction limit for UPI is ₹1,00,000 per day.
- **Special Categories:** For specific categories like capital markets, collections, insurance, and medical/educational services, the limit is extended up to ₹5,00,000.

### NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer)
An electronic funds transfer system maintained by the RBI.
- **Timing:** Operates in half-hourly batches, 24x7.
- **Limits:** No minimum or maximum limits for most bank accounts.

### RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)
Used for large value transactions requiring immediate clearing.
- **Timing:** Processed continuously in real-time, 24x7.
- **Limits:** Minimum amount to be remitted is ₹2,00,000. No upper ceiling.

### IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)
An instant payment inter-bank electronic funds transfer system.
- **Timing:** 24x7 real-time transfers.
- **Limits:** Maximum limit is generally ₹5,00,000 per transaction (varies by bank).

## Zero Liability Policy
According to RBI guidelines, customers have zero liability for unauthorized electronic banking transactions if they notify their bank within 3 working days of receiving the alert. If reported between 4-7 days, customer liability is capped.
