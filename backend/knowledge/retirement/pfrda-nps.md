# Retirement Planning and PFRDA

The Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) is the statutory body regulating the pension sector in India.

## National Pension System (NPS)
NPS is a voluntary, defined contribution retirement savings scheme designed to enable systematic savings. It is highly tax-efficient and low-cost.

### Types of Accounts
- **Tier I Account:** The mandatory retirement account. It comes with strict withdrawal rules and a lock-in period until age 60. Contributions here qualify for tax deductions.
- **Tier II Account:** A voluntary savings facility available only if you have an active Tier I account. It has no lock-in period, allowing you to withdraw money anytime. However, there are no tax benefits for Tier II contributions (except for Central Govt employees under specific conditions).

## Tax Benefits of NPS
NPS offers the highest tax deduction limits among all Indian investment instruments:
- **Section 80CCD(1):** Deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakh (part of the overall 80C limit).
- **Section 80CCD(1B):** An **exclusive additional deduction of ₹50,000** for voluntary contributions to NPS Tier I. This makes the total potential deduction ₹2.0 Lakhs.
- **Section 80CCD(2):** Employer's contribution up to 10% of salary (Basic + DA) is deductible over and above the ₹1.5 Lakh limit.

## Withdrawal Rules at Age 60
When you turn 60, you must utilize at least 40% of your total NPS corpus to purchase an annuity (a regular pension) from a life insurance company. The remaining 60% can be withdrawn as a lump sum, completely tax-free.
