# Insurance Basics and IRDAI Guidelines

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) regulates and promotes the insurance and re-insurance industries in India.

## Life Insurance Types

### Term Life Insurance
The purest and cheapest form of life insurance. It provides a massive life cover for a specific term. If the insured passes away during the term, the nominee gets the sum assured. If the insured survives the term, there is no maturity benefit (unless it's a Return of Premium plan). **Highly recommended for all breadwinners.**

### Endowment Plans / ULIPs
These combine insurance with investment. They charge higher premiums than term plans and offer a maturity benefit. However, they are often criticized for providing inadequate life cover and lower returns compared to separate investments in mutual funds.

## Health Insurance
Health insurance covers medical and surgical expenses. IRDAI has mandated standard health insurance policies like 'Arogya Sanjeevani' to ensure basic coverage across all insurers.

## Key IRDAI Regulations for Consumers

### Free-look Period
IRDAI mandates a 15-day free-look period (30 days for electronic/distance marketing policies). During this time, if you disagree with the terms and conditions of a life or health insurance policy, you can cancel it and receive a refund of your premium (subject to minor deductions).

### Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR)
CSR is the percentage of claims an insurer has paid out against the total claims received. A higher CSR (generally >95%) indicates a reliable insurer. IRDAI publishes these statistics annually.
