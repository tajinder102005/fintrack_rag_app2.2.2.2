# Budgeting Principles

Budgeting is the foundation of personal finance. It is the process of creating a plan to spend your money. This spending plan is called a budget. Creating this spending plan allows you to determine in advance whether you will have enough money to do the things you need to do or would like to do.

## The 50/30/20 Rule
A popular budgeting method is the 50/30/20 rule:
- **50% Needs**: Essential expenses like rent, groceries, utilities, and insurance.
- **30% Wants**: Non-essential spending like dining out, entertainment, and hobbies.
- **20% Savings & Debt**: Money put towards savings, investments, or paying down debt beyond minimum payments.

## Zero-Based Budgeting
Another method is zero-based budgeting, where your income minus your expenses equals zero. Every rupee is assigned a specific job before the month begins, ensuring maximum control over your money.

## Benefits of Budgeting
- Helps achieve financial goals faster.
- Prevents overspending.
- Reduces financial stress by providing clarity on where money is going.
- Highlights areas where you can cut back on unnecessary expenses.
