# Fraud Awareness and RBI Safety Guidelines

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) conducts continuous public awareness campaigns (like "RBI Kehta Hai") to educate consumers about financial frauds and safe banking practices.

## Common Financial Frauds

### Phishing and Vishing
- **Phishing:** Scammers send fake emails/SMS pretending to be a bank, asking you to click malicious links and enter credentials.
- **Vishing:** Fraudsters call posing as bank officials/police asking for your OTP, CVV, or PIN under the pretext of blocking a card or completing KYC.
- **RBI Rule:** Banks will NEVER call and ask for your PIN, OTP, CVV, or passwords.

### KYC Update Frauds
Scammers send SMS stating your bank account will be blocked unless you complete KYC via an attached link. Clicking the link and entering details compromises your account. Always use the official bank app or visit the branch for KYC.

### Screen Sharing Scams
Fraudsters convince you to download screen-sharing apps (like AnyDesk, TeamViewer) to "help" you resolve an issue. Once installed, they can see your screen, read your OTPs, and steal your funds.

## How to Protect Yourself
- Never share your OTP, PIN, or CVV with anyone.
- Do not click on unverified links in SMS or emails.
- Never download remote access apps on the instruction of unknown callers.
- Always type the bank's official URL in the browser instead of clicking search engine ads.

## Reporting Cyber Fraud
If you are a victim of financial cyber fraud in India:
- Immediately call the **National Cyber Crime Reporting Helpline: 1930**.
- File a complaint at **cybercrime.gov.in**.
- Block your cards/accounts through your bank's official app or customer care.
